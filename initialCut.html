05 / MULTIDISCIPLINARY CASE STUDY

PRIVATE RESIDENCE, TRICHY

CLIENT: PRIVATE FAMILY
COMPLETE MK ECOSYSTEM INTEGRATION

Designed as an urban sanctuary shielded from roadside heat and noise, this 7,800 sq.ft residence showcases the undeniable power of MK Group's unified delivery model.

PHASE 01: MK CONSTRUCTIONS

Seismic Raft & Thermal Mass Casting

Excavated 3.2m into stable hard strata. Formed an integrated waterproof raft foundation and cast 400mm thick thermally insulated concrete exterior envelope walls to drastically lower tropical heat transfer.

PHASE 02: MK PRECISION WINDOWS

Acoustic Thermal Double Glazing

Because window tolerances were planned concurrently with the concrete casting, customized floor-recessed uPVC sliding envelopes were installed with zero post-construction hacking, achieving 40dB noise reduction.

PHASE 03: MK DESIGN INTERRIORS

Seamless Teak Millwork & Indirect Lighting

Pre-planned wall recesses enabled flush, handle-less timber cabinetry and hidden ductwork, delivering an uninterrupted, quiet aesthetic from room to room.
